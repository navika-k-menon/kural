# Kural Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 29 September 2026*

Kural is built so that your data stays on your computer. Kural has no servers, no accounts, no tracking, no analytics and no advertising. The creator of Kural never receives any of your data.

- **Your voice** is processed on your computer only while you hold the dictation key (on Mac by Apple's on-device speech engine; on Windows by the open-source Whisper model). Audio is never recorded to a file, stored, or sent anywhere. On Windows, the speech model is downloaded once from Hugging Face on first use; no personal data is sent.
- **Your dictated text** is typed into the app you are using. If History is on, a copy is saved on your computer only, with the time and the name of the app, and is deleted automatically after the period you choose (off, 1, 7 or 30 days; 7 by default). You can delete items or all history at any time.
- **The clipboard** is used briefly to type your text and then restored. Dictated text is marked so clipboard-history tools don't keep it.
- **Self-corrections (Mac)** are processed by Apple Intelligence on your Mac.
- **Your date of birth** is used only to confirm you are 18 or older and is not saved.
- **Your preferences and settings** are stored only on your computer.

**Your rights:** you can view, copy and delete your history at any time, and "Delete all my data" removes everything Kural has stored. Because nothing is sent to us, no copy of your data exists anywhere else.

Kural is for people aged 18 and over.
