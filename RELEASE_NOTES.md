## Kural 0.2.0

Private, on-device voice dictation for Mac and Windows.

**New in 0.2.0**
- Personal dictionary: learns names and non-English words automatically, and learns from words you fix right after dictating
- Starter pack of common Indian words
- "Copy text" pop-up when there's no text box to type into
- Faster on Windows: transcribes while you talk, plus a new faster default speech model

**Also included**
- Hold-to-talk with a key of your choice, or double-tap for hands-free
- Voice commands, "strike that", automatic filler-word removal
- Self-correction on Mac using Apple's on-device AI
- Floating waveform pill, local history with auto-delete, 18+ onboarding

**Downloads**
- `Kural-Mac.zip`: Apple silicon Macs, macOS 26 or newer
- `Kural-Setup.exe`: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit)
